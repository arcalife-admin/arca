# Vendor Management Policy

**Owner:** ArcaLife Security Lead  
**Effective:** June 2026  
**Review:** Annual

---

## 1. Subprocessor approval

New subprocessors processing personal data require:
- Security assessment (SOC 2 / ISO 27001 preferred)
- DPA or equivalent contractual protections
- Update to [vendor-compliance.md](../../security/vendor-compliance.md) and customer DPA Annex B

| Sentry | SOC 2 Type II | June 2026 |

## 3. Ongoing monitoring

- Quarterly check of vendor trust centers for new reports or incidents
- Annual download of SOC 2 reports to internal compliance vault
- Vendor security advisories reviewed within 5 business days

## 4. Offboarding

When removing a subprocessor, ensure data deletion per vendor DPA and update subprocessor register.
