## Review schedule

All ISMS policies are reviewed **annually** or after a SEV-1/SEV-2 incident.

## SOC 2 alignment

These policies satisfy SOC 2 Common Criteria CC1 (control environment) and CC5 (control activities) for organizations pursuing Type II certification.
