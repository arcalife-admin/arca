# Acceptable Use Policy

**Owner:** ArcaLife Security Lead  
**Effective:** June 2026  
**Review:** Annual

---

## 1. Permitted use

ArcaLife systems may be used only for legitimate clinic management activities authorized by the employer (clinic or ArcaLife).

## 2. Prohibited activities

- Accessing patient data outside assigned organization or role
- Sharing credentials or upload station PINs
- Storing patient data on personal devices or unauthorized cloud services
- Circumventing access controls or audit logging
- Introducing malware or unauthorized software to production systems
- Using production data in development without anonymization

## 3. Remote access

Remote support follows [clinic-remote-setup.md](../../support/clinic-remote-setup.md) with GDPR-aware procedures.

## 4. Reporting

Report suspected policy violations or security incidents to security@arcalife.ro immediately.

## 5. Consequences

Violations may result in account suspension, contract termination, and legal action where applicable.
